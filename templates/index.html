Problem Statement

Phishing remains one of the most pervasive cyber threat vectors used by malicious actors to compromise corporate networks and steal user credentials.

Traditional security filters often fail against newly registered domains and obfuscated URLs. Users and organizations need rapid automated mechanisms to inspect links securely.

  • Billions lost annually to credential harvesting.
  • Zero-day phishing domains bypass static blocklists.
  • Manual link verification is slow & prone to error.
Proposed Solution

Our project delivers an automated heuristic scoring engine running on a secure Python backend that inspects URLs instantly.

  • Structural Anomaly Inspection: Parses excessive subdomains, `@` symbols, and IP hostnames.
  • SSL Socket Validation: Performs underlying cryptographic certificate checks.
  • Quantitative Risk Index: Calculates a 0-100 score categorizing links into Safe, Suspicious, or High Risk.
